# Privacy impact assessment (template)

Complete this before any operational use. It is not legal advice, and filling it in does not establish compliance with any law (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the GDPR).

1. **Purpose and necessity.** What decision does AEGIS support? Why is automated analysis necessary and proportionate?
2. **Lawful basis and notice.** The basis for processing each category of data subject (authors, depicted persons, speakers), and how they are informed.
3. **Data inventory.** Evidence types; personal data likely to be present (names in text, faces in images, voices in audio, EXIF GPS); case metadata; reviewer identities.
4. **Minimisation.** Collect only the necessary fields. AEGIS asks for no demographic data and performs no biometric identification. Use redaction for personal data not needed for the analysis.
5. **External processing.** Is any external provider enabled? Data-processing agreement, location, retention and sub-processors. The default is local-only processing.
6. **Retention.** Classifications, periods and legal holds (see the retention template).
7. **Access control.** Roles assigned, restricted cases, review of audit access.
8. **Accuracy and fairness.** Validated detectors and populations; subgroup error analysis; how inconclusive results are communicated.
9. **Rights and redress.** How affected people can contest findings (the appeal workflow); how corrections are made (new report versions).
10. **Security.** Reference the threat model and controls matrix, and record residual risks with their owners.
11. **Sign-off.** Data protection officer, legal and security approvers, and the review date.
